Win rules used

🔪 Mafia: wins when living Mafia equal or outnumber all other living players, unless a special independent role has already won.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Town (Civilians + good roles): wins when all Mafia are eliminated.

🃏 Joker: instantly wins if voted out during the day; does not win if killed at night.

💀 Serial Killer: wins if they are the sole survivor. This simple version checks this after each elimination.

These are default house rules. Doctor, Angel, Bodyguard, Witch, Mayor and Vigilante have their assigned roles but their special powers are not automatically resolved by the tracker; the host runs those rules manually.
